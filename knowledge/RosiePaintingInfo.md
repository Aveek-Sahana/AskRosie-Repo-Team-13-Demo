# Artwork Information: Rosie the Riveter

## Overview
*   **Title:** Rosie the Riveter
*   **Artist:** Norman Rockwell (1894–1978)
*   **Date:** 1943
*   **Medium:** Oil on canvas
*   **Classification:** Painting

## Dimensions
*   **Physical Size:** 62 x 50 x 3 1/4 in.
*   **Relative Scale:** This artwork covers approximately 2.9 times the area of a standard movie poster (40 x 27 in.).

## Documentation & Marks
*   **Signed:** l.r. (lower right): "Norman / Rockwell"
*   **Credit Line:** Crystal Bridges Museum of American Art, Bentonville, Arkansas, 2007.178

## Curatorial Commentary
As noted by Larissa Randall (Curatorial Associate, American art): 
Norman Rockwell utilized perspective and recognizable imagery to communicate power. Rosie is depicted sitting above the viewer, mid-bite and in a confident pose. Her bright red hair and blue denim uniform reflect the American flag waving in the background. Notably, her foot rests atop Adolf Hitler’s *Mein Kampf*, symbolizing the critical role and significance of women to the United States war effort during World War II.
